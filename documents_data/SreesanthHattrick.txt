The Indian Pace Bowler S Sreesanth has taken a Hattrick in his first ever match back after a hiatus of 10 years, that banned him from International and National cricket after spotfixing scandal. Sreesanth made a comeback to Jamaican T10 Tournament and wore a special jersey number 369, signifying the number of days his trial went by and he pleaded guilty. In his second over, Sreesanth, who played for Carrebian Carabaos ensured the pavillion for three players in three consecutive deliveries: K. Ngombo, L. Lupita Nyongo and Sikambo of Kingston Kings. Sreesanth's hattrick ensured victory for Carrebian Carabaos and ensured them a position in the next round of tournament, while Kingston Kings are now eliminated.
